Florence Wong

Hi, I'm Florence Mei Kuan Wong, an M.Sc. student in Mechanical Engineering at the Technical University of Berlin, specializing in mechanical design and development. My background includes hands-on work in heavy-industry gas turbines at Siemens Energy, automotive engineering at Mercedes-Benz, and applied research as a student research assistant at TU Berlin.

I'm passionate about the design and construction of complex engineering systems, from gas turbines and turbomachinery to aircraft engineering, and I currently research the dynamic behavior of bolted joint structures at Tsinghua University.

Outside of engineering, I speak five languages and enjoy connecting ideas across cultures and disciplines.

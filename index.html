I'm a master's student in mechanical engineering at TU Berlin, specialising in fluid and combustion engineering. My thesis looks at how bolted joints behave under vibration. Before that, I designed burner parts for large gas turbines at Siemens Energy.

Bolted joint structures

I'm modelling how bolted joints behave dynamically, at the Institute of Mechanics. The thesis is supervised at Tsinghua University and TU Berlin.

Research project

Hydrogen flame flashback

Flashback is when a flame travels back upstream into the burner. I'm studying it in hydrogen flames at the Institute of Fluid Dynamics and Technical Acoustics.

Lab project

Centrifugal pumps

I'm testing a radial centrifugal pump in forward and reverse operation and measuring how it performs in each direction.

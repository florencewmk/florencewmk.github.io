About me

Born and raised in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. Now an international student in Germany, and still looking up.

It started with the sky

For as long as I can remember I have been curious about the world, and even more about everything beyond it. At home in Kuala Lumpur my dad watched National Geographic all the time, so I grew up with the planet and the universe playing in our living room.

Then, in high school, I met a physics teacher who changed everything. Whatever I asked him, he had an answer. And when he didn't, he went and found one for me. That is what drove me into STEM for good.

The dream and the decision

I have always loved watching the sky. I wanted to know what is happening out there, far away from Earth, and for years my dream was to study astrophysics.

When the time came to choose, I chose engineering. I wanted to solve problems that people have here and now, in the real world. The stars did not go anywhere. I just look at them after work.

When I'm not studying

Photographing the night sky

I take astronomy photos: the Milky Way, the constellations and whatever else the sky shows me on a clear night.

Diving

I'm a PADI Rescue Diver. The sea is the other place I go to see a world that isn't ours.

Baking, cooking and moving

I bake, I cook and I swim regularly. I also only just learnt how to ride a bicycle.

Languages

Malaysia is a country of many cultures, so I grew up between languages. Today I speak English, Malay, Cantonese, Mandarin and German, and a little French.
